# Orion Chen’s personal website

## Platform

Web: Next.js App Router, React, content-collections. Chinese and English routes.

## Purpose

A personal home for code, documentary work, writing, and reading. Visitors can
browse published articles, discover works, read about the author, and leave a message.

## Confirmed commitments

The user approved a fullscreen retro pixel-art town in September 2026, replacing
the earlier paper-model direction. Use the existing `public/images/posts/orionchen.svg`
signature. Visitors enter writing, works, and reading rooms through shop doors;
the postbox opens the guestbook and the moving dog previews a random published article before the visitor chooses to read.
A notice beside the writing house links to the latest published article in the current language.
Two independently animated residents walk through the town. Preserve published content,
locale availability, routes, comments, and existing book recommendations. Never invent
finished projects or require personal photography.

The scene fills the viewport. Narrow screens pan horizontally; direct destination links
remain available. Approaching a door transitions into a matching pixel-art room at an
interior viewpoint. Click furniture to open content, then choose an article or archive.
Escape closes content first and then returns to the town. Pause and reduced-motion
settings stop ambient movement. The page uses layered 2D art, not free-roaming 3D.

Conventional archive and article pages retain readable editorial layouts and use matching
pixel room portraits. Homepage intro lists and footer stay removed. See `docs/pixel-town.md`
for the approved visual contract and implementation details.
