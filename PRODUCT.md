# Orion Chen’s personal website

## Platform

Web: Next.js App Router, React, content-collections. Chinese and English routes.

## Purpose

A personal home for code, documentary work, writing, and reading. Visitors can
browse published articles, discover works, read about the author, and leave a message.

## Confirmed commitments

In October 2026 the user retired the pixel town and chose a text-first personal site,
using antfu.me as the aesthetic reference: quiet typography, generous whitespace,
small personal details and polished interactions. The homepage introduces the author
through code, writing, film and reading. Keep the existing handwritten signature.
Preserve published content, bilingual routes, locale availability, comments and book
recommendations. No game or room-navigation experience remains on active pages.
